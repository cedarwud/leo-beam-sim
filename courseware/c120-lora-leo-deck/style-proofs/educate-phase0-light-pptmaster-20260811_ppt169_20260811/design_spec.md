## III. Visual Theme

### Theme Style

- **Mode**: custom
- **Visual style**: custom
- **Theme**: White institutional canvas with pale semantic fields, crisp navy typography, and small high-saturation process/energy/risk/service/condition accents
- **Tone**: Precise, teachable, bright, energetic, and technically bounded
- **Mode Behavior**: Advance in three proof acts—mechanism, conditional decision, and interpretation—so each page answers one question and hands a visible evidence chain to the next.
- **Visual Style Behavior**: Preserve the inherited Educate identity and its generous white field; stage each page as an open proof runway with one oversized editable relationship, asymmetric scale contrast, pale overlapping fields, thin navy connectors, and small saturated focal marks. Navy remains ink, hairline, and compact index—not a large filled plane. Avoid dashboard sidebars, equal-weight card grids, full-width filled rails, and decorative shadow stacks.

## IX. Content Outline

### Part 1: Causal proof

#### Slide 01 - Policy to endpoint evidence

- **Audience move**: See WAIT/SLEEP as a code label → understand the complete causal evidence chain and the service-first gate.
- **Layout**: Start from `templates/layout_layout_05.svg`. Keep its native Master/Layout atoms and title/page-number slots. Use a compact open policy card at the left, an oversized overlapping state/time field across the center, and unboxed interpretation notes at the bottom.
- **Title**: 一個策略選擇，如何變成可核對的端點能量？
- **Core message**: A marked policy edit matters only when it changes state/wake evidence and survives the service gate.
- **Content**: Left anchor: `只改一個地方` and `REST_DURING_GAP`. Dominant chain: `WAIT / SLEEP → state time (+ WAKE) → power × time → endpoint J → packet / service verdict`. WAIT means awake idle and no wake transition; SLEEP means low-power rest plus the first later active wake energy and latency. Interpretation rail: read service/deadline/freshness before endpoint energy. Failure rail: a null evidence change fails the consequential-change gate. Show the exact claim-boundary footer.
- **Visualization**: An editorial causal runway: WAIT/SLEEP orbit a dominant state-time lens, which opens into endpoint J and a service verdict. Secondary explanations remain annotations rather than peer cards.

#### Slide 02 - Conditional decision under a short window

- **Audience move**: Treat batching or urgency as a simple tweak → evaluate the decision path and freeze it before withheld evidence.
- **Layout**: Start from `templates/layout_layout_05.svg`. Keep the same title-only native Layout. Use a compact open policy card, a large crossing-window decision visual, and a thin freeze/withheld evidence lane.
- **Title**: 短窗口下，Queue、Deadline、Energy 要怎麼一起決定？
- **Core message**: Service conditions choose the action; endpoint efficiency cannot overrule a failed deadline or freshness gate.
- **Content**: Edit only `BATCH_SIZE` or `URGENT_MARGIN_S`. Decision path: contact closed → SLEEP; urgent due → SEND_URGENT; pacing gap → WAIT/SLEEP; quality ready → queue ≥ BATCH_SIZE ? FLUSH_BATCH : SEND_ONE; otherwise WAIT. Evidence gate: `baseline → candidate → one evidence-based revision → FREEZE → withheld`. Ledger: service/deadline/freshness; queue/packet/retry/expiry; state/wake/TX; endpoint J. Final rail: freeze before withheld, do not retune. Show the exact claim-boundary footer.
- **Visualization**: Two translucent contact-window beams cross at one large amber decision point; four ordered service gates and terminal actions attach to that visual, while freeze/withheld remains a thin evidence lane.

### Part 2: Bounded interpretation

#### Slide 03 - Service first, bounded claim last

- **Audience move**: Equate lower endpoint energy with success → state a complete, falsifiable, scope-bounded interpretation.
- **Layout**: Start from `templates/layout_layout_05.svg`. Keep the native title-only Layout. Make `Prediction ≠ Saving` the oversized editorial hero on white, followed by one continuous five-step evidence runway and an open statement field with COMPLETE/INCOMPLETE plus a falsifier.
- **Title**: 能量變好，不等於系統成功
- **Core message**: The defensible conclusion is `identity → service gate → mechanism → endpoint energy → bounded claim`.
- **Content**: Identity means the same scenario, policy lineage, case, seed, and freeze/provenance. Service means delivery, deadline, and freshness. Mechanism means queue, packet outcome, retry, and radio-state duration. Energy means endpoint-scope J breakdown; endpoint bit/J is valid only within that boundary. Interpretation is supports, contradicts, or insufficient. Closing statement: under a fixed scenario, policy, case, and endpoint energy boundary, state which mechanism changed which evidence, the service verdict, and only the resulting conditional claim. Add COMPLETE/INCOMPLETE and one falsifier; never fabricate KPI or completion. Show the exact claim-boundary footer.
- **Visualization**: Oversized editorial contrast plus an ordered evidence chain ending in a bounded claim, with service-pass and insufficient/falsified interpretations expressed by color, node scale, and line state rather than dark panels.
