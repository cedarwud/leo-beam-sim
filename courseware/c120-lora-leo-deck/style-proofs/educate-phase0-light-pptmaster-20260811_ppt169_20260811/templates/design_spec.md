# Educate C-120 Light Course Deck — Design Specification

## I. Template Overview

| Application context | Definition |
|---|---|
| Recurring presentation family | C-120 smart-energy and IoT teaching decks that connect a marked policy edit to mechanism, service evidence, and endpoint-energy interpretation. |
| Intended audiences and outcomes | Novice learners and instructors; the deck should make the causal chain and its claim boundary easy to explain, inspect, and reopen for review. |
| Delivery and reading assumptions | Classroom projection plus close-read review and handoff. |
| Representative narrative/page roles | Cover, mechanism explanation, conditional decision, evidence interpretation, and bounded closing. |

The preserved Educate identity is light and institutional: white canvas, pale watermark, purple divider, fixed top-right logo, and fixed footer. Project pages may translate the donor deck's visual grammar with one navy anchor and restrained semantic accents while leaving the inherited identity visible.

## II. Color Scheme

- Institutional purple: `#35377F` for inherited identity and page-level emphasis.
- White: `#FFFFFF` for the preserved canvas and primary reading surface.
- Deep navy: `#17223B` for one strong explanatory anchor on a light page.
- Cyan: `#28B8C7` for process and state-flow cues.
- Amber: `#F4B942` for energy and cost cues.
- Coral: `#EA6A5A` for risk, failure, or falsifier cues.
- Green: `#4EA978` for service-preserving outcomes.
- Violet: `#9B79D0` for conditional or withheld-evidence cues.
- Pale supporting fields use low-saturation tints of the semantic accents; color reinforces labels and geometry but is not the sole carrier of meaning.

## III. Typography

- Chinese identity and teaching text: `DFKai-SB`, `BiauKai`, `標楷體`, serif.
- Latin text and numerals: `Times New Roman`, serif.
- The generated course proof uses 28 pt titles and 24 pt principal teaching copy; smaller type is limited to captions, status labels, and the fixed claim-boundary footer.

## IV. Signature Design Elements

- Preserve the source Master/Layout topology, top-right logo, footer strip, page number, pale watermark, purple divider, and source placeholder geometry exactly.
- Keep the canvas white; do not add a slide-level background or obscure inherited identity with a full-slide shape.
- Within the content field, use one dominant mechanism or decision visual, one deep-navy anchor occupying roughly one quarter of the available body field, semantic accent rails, rounded rectangles, thin connectors, and deliberate white space.
- The recognizable reading rhythm is stage cue, core question, dominant visual, then interpretation/recovery. The generated proof may repeat an appropriate preserved Layout without implying source-slide order or source-slide count.
