# Unified visual lab final-screen prototype

Question: can one desktop surface make the complete multi-beam LEO energy lab
feel like a single, readable product before the live backend is connected?

This is a throwaway review surface at `/prototype/visual-lab-g0`. It must not
become another scientific producer or a second product route. The accepted
layout will later be folded into the unified product and the mock adapter will
be replaced by the canonical session.

## Checkable completion criterion

At 1440×900, one immediately usable page renders without an intro screen and:

- enters a calm progressive workspace by default, not a text-heavy guided
  lesson and not a binary simple/full fork;
- keeps the scene and a compact core result set visible while `場景資料`,
  `換手`, `SINR`, `Power`, `Throughput`, and `EE` modules can be opened and
  closed independently in a causal, task-oriented composition;
- allows each open scientific module to reveal its controls, matching outcomes,
  formula, explanation and source without exposing unrelated modules;
- offers one action to open the complete engineering workspace and one action
  to return to the calm core, while preserving all input, scene, selected UE,
  accepted source, time and deterministic snapshot state;
- treats a future detailed teaching guide as a separate experience that reuses
  these modules; it is not the default workspace disclosure mechanism;
- uses 16px-or-larger body text in the progressive workspace and keeps the scene
  as the visual priority instead of opening on two dense engineering rails;
- exposes all 17 canonical editable inputs through the four scientific modules,
  without making those controls the first-use experience;
- keeps a small set of legible outcome metrics visible in the calm core and
  progressively adds matching serving/candidate details as modules are opened;
- uses one 3D scene that can switch among Earth, NTPU sky and local service views,
  and among clean/context/full disclosure levels;
- renders visibly populated deterministic Starlink and OneWeb constellation
  shells around the Earth, so switching constellation changes a globe-scale
  spatial pattern rather than merely recolouring a few orbit lines;
- shows NTPU, seven cells, representative UEs, serving/candidate/context
  satellites, beams, interference, handover and energy flow in the same scene;
- lets the representative UE be moved inside the local service scene;
- aligns data, power, energy, EE and handover events on one bottom timeline;
- uses readable type (body 14px or larger; metadata 12px or larger);
- clearly states that the deterministic values are a front-end mock and are not
  TLE, canonical EE, measured, or citable evidence.

The prototype stops for owner review after this criterion is browser-checked.
