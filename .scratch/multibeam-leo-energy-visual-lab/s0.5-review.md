  `h^div = max(h, 1e-12)` helper.

  no duplicate U x B matrix was added.

  vector plus the fixture propagation/receive inputs.
- The zero-`h` oracle is pinned to `h=0`, `h^div=1e-12`,
  `p_req=71.77346253629314 W`, zero signal, and zero rate.

### 2. Same-link candidate identity and role

- The serving representative UE is selected once.
- The candidate projection must reuse the same `userIndex`, `userId`, and
  `beamId`; otherwise it is unavailable.
- The explicit role is `single-link-comparison` with no active beam ownership
  and no contribution to serving interference.
- Candidate scenario-level EE is no longer exposed as candidate-link EE; the
  candidate link value is unavailable (`null`).
- A malformed candidate that repeats the serving satellite identity fails
  closed as unavailable.

### 3. Immutable serving-change evidence

  `traceDigest`, pre-commit serving/candidate values, post-commit serving
  identity, and target pass provenance.

  qualification anchors.
- Forced-continuity evidence records invisible old serving, visible real
  target, and the source-backed continuity reason.
- Pre-commit serving identity and trigger instant are validated before/after
  state mutation.
- Target provenance must be one of the planner-selected serving/candidate
  passes at the trigger anchor. A merely overlapping geometry fallback cannot
  publish event evidence.
- The original 3 dB offset, 30 s TTT, 30 s decision axis, initial-attach count,
  event sequence, and cumulative counts remain unchanged.
