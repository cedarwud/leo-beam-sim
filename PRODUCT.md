# Product

## Register

product

## Current design scope

The design contract in this file applies to the isolated `/course/c120` learner
route. It does not redesign or redefine the legacy simulator routes.

## Users

Twenty non-communications STEM undergraduates use one laptop each in a bright
classroom. They may have no satellite, radio, networking, orbital, or simulator
background. They are following a two-hour instructor-led activity and must be
able to recover without debugging software.

## Product purpose

C-120 helps a learner make an energy decision, freeze a prediction, replay the
consequence, and transfer the reasoning to a non-satellite competition idea.
LEO is a concrete moving-service example, not a prerequisite subject. Success is
an understandable, reopenable workbook with eight bounded responses, not fluency
in simulator internals.

## Brand personality

Focused, exploratory, concrete. The route belongs to the same dark visual world
as the simulator, while remaining readable enough for a first classroom encounter.

## Anti-references

- Dense mission-control dashboards that foreground telemetry and provenance.
- Generic light SaaS cards or warm-paper classroom themes that erase the simulator's identity.
- Tiny labels, acronym walls, dense card grids, and several equally prominent actions.
- English-only technical copy or literal translations that retain unexplained jargon.
- Interfaces that expose every advanced detail before the learner needs it.
- Decorative animation, artificial sci-fi styling, or colour-only state changes.

## Design principles

1. One learner question and one obvious next action at a time.
2. Traditional Chinese is the first-run language; English is always one click away.
3. Introduce each unfamiliar idea through the decision being made, then disclose technical evidence on demand. Do not talk down to the learner or announce what background they lack.
4. Keep service, watts, joules, delivered data, and bit/J honest and visibly separate.
5. Preserve progress, recovery, provider identity, and claim boundaries without making them the visual headline.

## Accessibility & inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. The complete learner path must work by keyboard, retain visible
focus, provide redundant non-colour cues, support 200% zoom and 320 px width, and
keep form text at least 16 px. Body copy targets at least 18 px on classroom laptop
screens. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences. No student installation,
programming, or debugging is required.
